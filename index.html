Our Story

A Passion for
Flavor & Craft

Born from a love of honest ingredients and timeless technique, Savoria has been serving the city's most memorable meals since 2012.

Our kitchen is led by Chef Marco Bellini, whose philosophy is simple: respect the ingredient, honor the season, and never rush the plate. From our wood-fired oven to our candlelit dining room, every detail is designed to make you slow down and savor.
